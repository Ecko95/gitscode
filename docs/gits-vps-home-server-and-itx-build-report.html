Current PC Becomes The Server

The Ryzen 7 3700X mini ITX system is enough for GITS/HERMES. The GPU is the thing to remove or avoid using if power matters.

EUR 7-14 estimated monthly power

50 W always on is about 36 kWh/month. At EUR 0.20/kWh, that is about EUR 7.20/month. At 100 W, it is about EUR 14.40/month.

  • Enable BIOS C-states and Ryzen Eco Mode or lower PPT.
  • Remove the RTX 3080 if the machine can boot headless or with a tiny display adapter.
  • Run Linux headless, no desktop environment.
  • Use SSDs for repos, state, logs, and databases.
  • Add a small UPS if HERMES becomes important.
  • Keep offsite backups even after moving home.

RTX 3080 Reality Check

The RTX 3080 is about 5 years and 10 months old as of this report. It is useful, but it should not be the always-on server GPU if energy saving matters.

Launch date 2020-09-17

NVIDIA launched the RTX 3080 first in the RTX 30 series.

Still good 1440p

Useful for gaming and CUDA work that fits inside 10-12 GB VRAM.

Weak spot VRAM

Newer games, local AI, and creator workloads want more headroom.

Recommendation Reuse

Keep it until RTX 5080 pricing is attractive, then upgrade.

CPU Benchmark Class

The 3700X is not bad; it is just out of class for a 2026 5K2K high-refresh desktop with heavy dev workloads.

CPU Cores / threads PassMark class
Ryzen 7 3700X 8 / 16 About 22k. Baseline; dated for high-refresh 5K2K and heavy multitasking.
Ryzen 7 9800X3D 8 / 16 About 40k. Huge gaming uplift, efficient, easier to cool.
Ryzen 9 9950X3D 16 / 32 About 69k. Best all-round pick for gaming plus compile, VM, container, and agent workloads.

GPU Benchmark Target For 5K2K

Once the CPU/RAM bottleneck is removed, the monitor is GPU-bound. Treat 5K2K as heavier than 4K, not as normal ultrawide.

GPU VRAM / power Expected 5K2K role
RTX 3080 10-12 GB, about 320 W Usable today, especially with DLSS, but VRAM and ray tracing are the weak spots.
RTX 5080 16 GB, 360 W, 850 W PSU guidance Best sane upgrade target: roughly 50-70% faster than RTX 3080 in many 4K-class comparisons, plus DLSS 4/MFG.
RTX 5090 32 GB, 575 W, 1000 W PSU guidance Best raw headroom for 5K2K, AI, and creator work, but current pricing and NR200P fit make it a bad default.

NR200P Cooling And Fit

The case works, but the exact cooler and GPU model matter. This is where a PCPartPicker-compatible list still needs manual clearance checks.

Item Fit verdict Decision
Thermalright Phantom Spirit 120 SE air Fits: 154 mm cooler vs 155 mm NR200P limit. Best simple option; use mesh side panel, tune 9950X3D Eco/PBO if needed.
Noctua NH-U12A Does not officially fit: 158 mm cooler vs 155 mm limit. Skip for this case unless you want to gamble or mod.
280 mm AIO Supported by NR200P, but layout matters. Best sustained-load choice for 9950X3D if side-radiator/GPU layout works.
RTX 5080 Likely with a compact/SFF-friendly model. Check card length, width, slot thickness, and 12V-2x6 cable bend before buying.
RTX 5090 Not a default fit for original NR200P. Only buy after confirming exact card dimensions; many partner cards are too large or too hot.
Corsair Platinum 750 W Fine for RTX 3080 stage. Upgrade to SF1000 for RTX 5080/5090; NVIDIA/Corsair guidance is 850 W for 5080, 1000 W for 5090.
